# Decision log and handoff document

## Log to keep during the exchange

A silent trace, never reported turn by turn:

- how the need evolved and the moments that changed the direction;
- criteria, constraints and preferences expressed;
- assumptions confirmed, refuted or still uncertain;
- approaches considered, rejected or deferred, with their reason;
- disagreements raised and the user's answer;
- scenarios, edge cases, research and sources used;
- decisions made and the reasons behind them.

## Handoff document

A reliable handoff for another agent: what was decided, why, and what must not be rediscovered. Neither a verbatim transcript nor an arbitrarily detailed implementation plan. Depth fitted to the topic.

1. **Header**: topic, date, status of the thinking, related context or repository.
2. **Executive summary**: final need, recommended direction, main reasons.
3. **How the thinking evolved**: the steps that materially changed the need.
4. **Goals and non-goals**: intended outcome and explicit limits.
5. **Criteria and constraints**: user, product, business, technical, operational.
6. **Compared approaches**: table of options, benefits, costs, risks, conditions for choosing each.
7. **Decisions made**: decision, rationale, expected consequences.
8. **Rejected or deferred options**: precise reason and any condition for revisiting.
9. **Disagreements and trade-offs**: points that were challenged and how they were resolved.
10. **Scenarios and edge cases**: examples used to test the direction.
11. **Research and evidence** (if research was done): linked sources, lessons, limits, inferences.
12. **Risks, unknowns and open questions**: without hiding the remaining uncertainties.
13. **Handoff brief**: expected outcome, useful context, constraints, observable acceptance criteria for the next agent. No file, API or task that was not validated.

Internal navigation, and tables for comparisons. Visually distinguish decisions, assumptions, rejected items and open questions. Summarize faithfully while keeping the decisive reasoning.

## Checks before delivery

- No important decision or rejected option is missing, and no awkward uncertainty is left out to make the conclusion look better.
- No fact presented as a decision, no inference as proof, no idea that was merely discussed presented as a validated requirement.
- HTML opened or rendered to check readability and navigation; file fixed if needed.
